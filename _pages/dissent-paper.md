title: "Dissent in central bank communication"

This paper studies how dissent in central bank communication affects the outcomes of monetary policy. By assembling a novel and uniquely rich data set on the proceedings of the Swedish central bank, the Riksbank, I provide new insights about dissent in central banking. I map dissent to governor demographics and highlight some characteristics co-varying with dissent. Furthermore, I find that dissent in sentiment correlates strongly with dissent in voting and on the interest rate path. I also show that greater dissent within the board reduces the effects on short term interest rates by about 33%. When studying monetary policy shocks from transcripts in different dissent regimes, we also see that dissent can have an effect on the shape of the interest rate path.
